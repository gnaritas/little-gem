The oven

Wood-fired pizza, Madison Park

4235 E Madison Street has pulled wood-fired pies for years. Little Gem is bringing the fire back — and the takeout counter will be the first thing to glow.

A wood-fired address

Nearly 17 years of fire

Wood-fired pizza is what The Independent Pizzeria made here for nearly 17 years — it’s how the corner became the neighborhood’s spot, and it’s the practice Little Gem is carrying on. The new team said it plainly in its own first posts: the oven “will be fired up again soon.”

wood-fired practice and tenure per the Seattle Met business directory and What Now Seattle; oven promise per Little Gem on Instagram, Oct 30, 2026

Wood-fired, done right, means a hot oven, fast cooks and a crust with char on the ridge. The dough going into it is the neighborhood’s own — handed down recipe and technique from the kitchen that made it famous.
