The lineage of a neighborhood pie

When The Independent Pizzeria closed in October 2026 after nearly 17 years, the plan was already in motion: its owners are teaching Little Gem’s team the recipes and techniques that made the place — the dough included.

That is the whole idea of Little Gem: not a new pizza brand, but the neighborhood’s pizza, carried on — from the same wood-fired oven, at the same address, with a short, thoughtful drinks list beside it.
