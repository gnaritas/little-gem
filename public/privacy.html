The short version

Privacy, plainly

This site collects as little as it can. What you leave here stays with the shop.

Contact the shopFront page

What gets kept

Fill in the contact form and the shop receives your name, email, phone (if you left one) and your message. That’s it. It’s used to answer you and kept by the Little Gem team — not shared, not sold, not used for advertising.

The map image is served by Google Maps, which sees your IP address while the map loads. The web host also logs routine technical details (IP address, browser type) to keep the site running.

What doesn’t happen

No advertising networks. No tracking scripts. No analytics cookies. No third-party pixels. Nothing you type here builds a profile about you.

To see or have removed anything held from you, say the word on the contact page.
