Everything we know

Madison Park’s pizza questions, answered

Short answers with sources — and honest blanks: anything the shop hasn’t announced yet is left pending rather than guessed.

When is Little Gem opening?

Takeout first: the owner told What Now Seattle that takeout is hoped to start in December 2026 — no dates set — with the full opening planned for early 2027. When a date is real, it goes out on @littlegemseattle first.

Where exactly is Little Gem?

4235 E Madison Street in Madison Park, Seattle (98112) — the corner home of The Independent Pizzeria for its final nearly 17 years. The address was confirmed by the owner to What Now Seattle.

Is it the same pizza as the old Independent Pizzeria?

That’s the plan, right down to the dough: the Independent Pizzeria’s former owners are teaching Little Gem’s team their recipes and techniques, dough recipe included (What Now Seattle, citing The Seattle Times). Little Gem put it best in its first post: “It’s an honor to continue to offer your pizzas to the community.”

Who is behind Little Gem?

A veteran of Seattle’s bar and restaurant scene — a 2026 James Beard Award semifinalist for Outstanding Professional in Cocktail Service — stepping behind the pizza peel. Full introductions land with the menu announcement.

Will the pizza be wood-fired?

Yes — 4235 E Madison Street is a wood-fired pizza kitchen, and Little Gem’s own account promised the oven “will be fired up again soon.”

Is there a menu yet?

Not yet. Pie names, prices, hours and ordering channels get published when the shop itself announces them, on Instagram first. This site deliberately doesn’t guess.

What will you pour?

Wine and low-alcohol cocktails alongside the pizza, per the owner’s interview with What Now Seattle.

How do I hear about opening day first?

Two front doors: follow @littlegemseattle on Instagram, or leave a note on the contact page — dates, menu and ordering news land there first.
